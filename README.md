Dear all people,

The ZIP files contain the plans for building the robot of stab with FUSION, to set up the prototype you need :

First two aluminium blocks of 40x300x400 mm for example sold on the ebay,

Secondly 4 bearings of 22 mm, 1 axis of 8 mm and also few circlips to make up the 2-axis mechanical link for example sold on mchobby and manomano,

Also 2 screws of M8 of 80 mm long to fix the Bâti to the Entretoise and 2 axis hub to fix the motors to the Support,

Moreover you will need few screws of M3 of different lengths to mount the two motors and the two supports on the shaft of the Robot named the Bras and the central axis of the Robot.

Thirdly 2 motors with reductors of 1:200 for example sold on conrad,

Then you need to tell to someone equipped with the machine for drilling and fraising the matter of Aluminum AlMg3 aw 5754 and to make the sphere in inox for a good sliping surface ...

... to make a Robot with someone who has the machines to drill and to mill the matter of Aluminium AlMg3 aw 5754.

Finally you can mount the Bras, the Bâti, the Entretoise and the central Axe with the Support of the two motors with reductors.

BR, EO.

NB :  Please take care of the aluminium AU4G to make a prototype that is better to turn.

PS :  Personally I am using to build the robot the makerbeam XL to set up the Portant and the feet of the robot.
